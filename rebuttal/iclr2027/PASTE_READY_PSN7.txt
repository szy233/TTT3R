Tests are paired over sequences (Wilcoxon, bootstrap CI, no multiplicity correction). "Better" needs p<0.05 and a gap above 2.2%, and anything else is tied, which does not show equivalence. A 1e-7 change to one multiplier (one draw) moved means by up to 4.7% (TUM ATE), 6.9% (KITTI ATE) and 13.4% (KITTI segment rotation error), so smaller gaps are within numerical noise (revised PDF, A.12). So yes, it changes part of the conclusion. Less pace alone beats TTT3R on TUM (8/8) and ScanNet (46/65), and ties it on ARKitScenes (p=0.59). At ortho's own norm schedule, direction helps on TUM, at higher mean RPE rotation, and hurts on ScanNet. On KITTI Odometry, at ortho's norm schedule, direction lowers Sim(3)-aligned segment rotation error (11.88 vs 16.78 deg/100m, 10/11, p=0.019; metric chosen after a scalar comparison), while ATE and segment translation tie and RPE translation worsens (0/11). At const α's per-token step length, direction lowers KITTI segment rotation error at α=0.10, 0.20 and 0.33 (7.68 vs 12.41, 7.88 vs 16.15, 9.13 vs 17.61, p≤0.014), with worse ATE at 0.10 and RPE translation at 0.10 and 0.20.
